## Long Description

### SLA Pulse for Jira — Real-Time SLA Monitoring, Breach Prediction & Root-Cause Insights

Stop finding out about SLA breaches after they happen. PulseRook gives your team a live, color-coded view of every ticket's SLA health — with rule-based risk scoring, breach prediction, and root-cause diagnostics — right inside Jira. No external AI. No data leaving Atlassian's cloud. No per-call costs.

### What you get:

**Executive Health Score** — A single 0–100 score (A–F grade) blending compliance, risk, MTTR, reopen rate, and workload. Leadership gets an instant read on team health.

**Breach Prediction** — Every ticket gets a risk score and level (Healthy / Moderate / At Risk / Critical) based on elapsed time, priority, and status. A dedicated panel shows what will slip before it does.

**"Why Are We Missing SLAs?"** — An automatic root-cause engine diagnoses the top drivers behind breaches (status bottlenecks, assignee overload, priority misclassification, slow first response, chronic backlog) and gives concrete recommendations.

**Custom SLA Rules** — Set your own SLA hours per priority level (Highest/High/Medium/Low/Lowest), applied instantly across the whole app.

**Slack Alerts & Daily Digest** — Near-breach warnings, breach alerts, and a daily summary posted straight to your team's Slack channel.

**Reports & Export** — CSV and PDF export of SLA performance, priority breakdowns, and executive summaries for stakeholder reporting.

**Insights** — Weekly trends, risk hotspots, bottleneck detection, assignee performance, and reopen-rate analysis.

**SLA Health by Group** — Filter by Jira group to see SLA compliance per team. One click to drill into a specific team's health.

**Every metric is clickable** — Jump straight from any count, chart, or table row to the exact filtered list of Jira issues behind it.

### Why teams choose PulseRook:

- **Fully rule-based** — Deterministic, explainable, and fast. No AI black box. No API keys. No per-token costs.
- **Built natively on Atlassian Forge** — Runs inside Jira. No separate hosting or login.
- **Set up in minutes** — Install, pick a project, set SLA hours. Your dashboard is live.
- **Admin-only configuration** — Only Jira admins can change SLA rules and notification settings. Regular users see read-only dashboards.
- **Security-first** — All data stays in Jira and Atlassian Forge. No third-party API calls except optional Slack webhooks.
